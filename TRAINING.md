# Thai Boran app — how to use it

This is for the people at the front desk and the manager. You do not need to
know anything about computers to follow it. Read the part that is your job.

The app has four tabs across the top:

| Tab | Who uses it | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiver Form** | everyone | Taking a new client's waiver |
| **Front desk** | receptionist | Checking the day and making the documents |
| **Manager** | manager only | Approving documents |
| **Sales & history** | both | Approved documents, sales, past clients |

The **Waiver Form** tab is the one the client sees. It is the tab the iPad
should be left on. It shows nothing about money or other clients.

---

# Part 1 — For the receptionist

## Your day, in order

### 1. Take each client's waiver

On the **Waiver Form** tab:

1. Type the client's **name**, **contact number** and **address**.
2. Choose the **massage** and any **add-ons** they are having.
3. Choose the **therapist** and the **time it starts**.
4. Tick any **medical conditions** the client tells you about.
5. Tick the **Privacy Notice** box.
6. Press **Take a Photo** and take the client's photo.
7. Hand the iPad to the client and ask them to **sign** in the white box.
8. Press **Submit**.

The app tells you if something is missing and what to do about it. For
example, if the client has not signed yet it says so and asks you to have
them sign before pressing Save again.

**If a client does not want their photo taken**, the switch is right there on
the waiver screen, by the photo step. Tap the green bar that says **Photos
ON**, then tap what you want. Three choices:

- **Photograph every client** — normal.
- **Skip the photo for THIS client only** — this one client, then it goes
  back to normal by itself.
- **Stop until I turn it back on** — stays off, even if the iPad is
  restarted, until you turn it back on.

Tap your choice and it takes effect straight away: the menu closes by itself,
the bar says “saved” for a moment, and from then on the bar shows which one is
on without you opening anything. This bar is the ONLY place the photo setting
lives — there is nothing to find in another tab.

### 2. Check today's sessions

Go to the **Front desk** tab. It opens on **Today**.

Every waiver you took today is listed. Look down the list and check the
therapist, the hours, the stub number and how the client paid.

If something is wrong, press **Edit** on that line, fix it, and press
**Save**. If you add an add-on the client asked for during the massage, the
price and the hours change by themselves — you do not have to work anything
out.

> Fixing a session here fixes it everywhere: the documents you make
> afterwards will already have the correct figure.

### 3. Make the day's documents

Still on the **Front desk** tab, press **Documents**.

1. Choose which document you are making.
2. Choose the **date**. For the weekly ones just pick any day in that week —
   the app uses the whole week, Monday to Sunday.
3. Press **Create / refresh from records**. The document appears, filled in
   from the waivers you took.

The four documents are:

- **Therapist Daily Commission Report** — one day
- **Therapist Weekly Commission Report** — one week
- **Sales Weekly Report** — one week
- **Main Office Daily Sales Report** — one day

### 4. Correct anything that is wrong

Press **✎ Correct values on the document**. The boxes you can change turn
pale yellow. Type straight into them.

**Important:** correcting one box never changes another. If you change the
hours from 1 to 2, the money stays exactly as it was — the app does not
guess. Only the totals at the bottom add themselves up again.

Press **Save changes** when you are done.

If a box turns pink, what you typed is not a number the app can use. Fix it
and save again.

### 5. Sign it and send it

Press **Submit for approval**.

A box opens with your name in it. **Sign with your finger** in the white
space, then press **Save & submit**.

- **Clear** wipes the signature so you can sign again.
- **Cancel** closes the box and sends nothing.

You must sign — the document cannot go to the manager without it. Your
signature is printed on the finished document next to the manager's.

If you change the document afterwards, you sign again. That is normal: the
signature belongs to the version you sent.

### 6. Later — see what the manager approved

**Sales & history** tab → **Approved documents**. Anything the manager has
signed off is there, exactly as it was approved. You can open it, print it,
or save it as a PDF.

# Part 2 — For the manager

## Signing in

Open the **Manager** tab and sign in.

Capital letters in your username do not matter — `Boss`, `boss` and `BOSS`
are all the same account. The password must be typed exactly.

If a receptionist opens the Manager tab, she sees a screen saying manager
access needs a manager login. That is normal and nothing is broken.

## Your day, in order

### 1. See what is waiting

The Manager tab opens on **To approve**. A red number on that button tells
you how many documents are waiting, from both parlors.

### 2. Review a document

Press **Review** on a line. The document opens exactly as the receptionist
sent it, with her signature already on it.

Read it. If something is wrong, do **not** try to change it here — documents
are made and corrected at the front desk. Ask the receptionist to fix it and
send it again.

### 3. Approve it

Press **Approve ✓**. Your stored signature and today's date are put on it,
and a permanent copy is saved that can never be changed.

If a correction is needed later, the receptionist makes a new version. The
approved copy you signed stays exactly as it is, forever.

### 4. Find documents afterwards

**In the app:** **Sales & history** tab → **Approved documents**. Filter by
document and by date. Press **Open** to read it or **PDF** to save it.

**In Google Drive:** approved documents are filed automatically:

```
<Parlor> / <Year> / <Month> / Daily   or  Weekly
```

For example a daily sheet for 20 August 2026 at Panacan is in
`Panacan / 2026 / August / Daily`.

### 5. Prices, staff, users and codes

Manager tab → **Admin**:

- **Prices** — what each massage and add-on costs. Shared by both parlors.
- **TOM codes** — the short code (BS, TS, BK, TB, TA, F …) printed on the
  Main Office Daily Sales Report. Any service without a code is listed at the
  top of that panel; give it one and the next document uses it.
- **Therapists** — add, edit, deactivate.
- **Users & passwords** — reset a receptionist's password.
- **Google Drive mirror** — whether uploads are working.

# Part 3 — When something goes wrong

## "Update available" appears at the bottom of the screen

This is how the app updates itself. **You never delete the app or install it
again.**

- Press **Update now** and wait a few seconds. The app reloads on the new
  version. Nothing is lost.
- Press **Later** if a client is standing in front of you. It comes back in a
  few minutes.

If you press **Update now** while a waiver is half-filled, the app will not
reload. It tells you what to finish first. Finish it, then press **Update
now** again.

## "Server is waking up"

The server goes to sleep when nobody has used it for a while, to keep it
free. The first thing you do after a quiet period wakes it up, which takes up
to a minute.

**What to do:** wait. It wakes up by itself. Nothing is lost.

## The WiFi has dropped

- **The Waiver Form keeps working.** Carry on taking waivers exactly as
  normal. They are saved on the iPad.
- When the WiFi comes back they are sent by themselves. The chip at the top
  right shows **Offline**, then how many are waiting, then **All synced**.
- **Front desk, Manager and Sales & history need WiFi.** They will say so
  instead of showing half-right numbers.

**Never delete the app while waivers are waiting to be sent** — that is the
only way to lose them.

## "That username or password was not recognised"

- Capital letters in the username do not matter. The password must be exact.
- Check the keyboard has not typed a capital first letter into the password.
- If you have forgotten the password, the manager resets it: Manager tab →
  **Admin** → **Users & passwords** → **Reset password**. He gives you a
  temporary password, you sign in with it, and the app asks you to choose
  your own.
- **The manager's own password** cannot be reset in the app. Contact Alberto.

## "You have been signed out"

The back office signs you out by itself after about three minutes of not
being touched, and goes back to the Waiver Form tab. This is on purpose: it
stops a client picking up the iPad and reading the day's takings.

**What to do:** sign in again. Nothing is lost.

## A document will not send

The message says what is wrong. The usual ones:

- **"The document has to be signed"** — press Submit again and sign.
- **"That document has not been made yet"** — press *Create / refresh from
  records* first.
- **"The manager has already approved this"** — press *Create / refresh from
  records* to start a new version.

## The document runs off the side of the screen

It does not — it is scaled to fit. Use the **−**, **+** and **Fit width**
buttons above it to zoom, and drag sideways when zoomed in.

## The app looks like the old version

If a screen looks like it did before an update — the wrong layout, a button
that should be there is missing — the iPad is holding on to old files.

**Do this, in order. Stop as soon as it looks right.**

1. **Close the app properly and open it again.** Swipe up from the bottom of
   the iPad, flick the Thai Boran app card away, then tap the icon again.
   This alone fixes it almost every time.
2. **Scroll to the bottom of any screen and tap "Check for updates".** If a
   new version is waiting, the blue bar appears — tap **Update now**.
3. **Tap "Reinstall app files"** (next to it at the bottom of the screen).
   Say yes. The app fetches a fresh copy from the server and opens again.

> **Waivers on the iPad are not touched by any of these.** Reinstalling app
> files replaces the app, not your records.

Only if all three fail: tell Alberto the version number shown at the top
right of the screen. **Do not delete the app icon** — that is no longer the
fix, and if any waivers have not been sent yet it is the one thing that can
lose them.

## Which version is this iPad on?

Two places, whichever is easier:

- **Top right of the screen**, always visible, next to Online/Offline:
  a small `v2026.08.20-05`.
- **Bottom of any screen**, with the buttons:

```
Thai Boran app · version 2026.08.20-05   [ Check for updates ]  [ Reinstall app files ]
```

Read that number out if you are asked on the phone. **Check for updates**
makes the iPad look for a new version straight away.

# Who to contact

**Alberto** — anything the app does that this guide does not explain, a
manager password reset, or anything that looks broken.

When you contact him, it helps a lot to say:

1. **Which tab** you were on (Waiver Form, Front desk, Manager, Sales &
   history).
2. **What you pressed.**
3. **Exactly what the message said** — read it out or send a photo of the
   screen.
4. **The version number** from the bottom of the screen.

Nothing you can press in this app deletes a client's record. If you are not
sure, stop and ask — it is always safer than guessing.
